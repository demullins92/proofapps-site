Proof Apps LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the BottleCall mobile application and web dashboard (together, the "Service"). BottleCall helps bars and restaurants take voice- and camera-based liquor inventory counts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when your business uses BottleCall.

By using BottleCall, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Who this applies to: BottleCall is used in two ways — by managers, who create a real account to set up their location, catalog, staff, and view reports on the web dashboard; and by staff members, who use a shared location access code plus a personal PIN on the mobile app to run inventory counts, with no personal account or sign-up required.

1. Information We Collect

Manager Account Information
When a manager creates a BottleCall account, we collect an email address and password, stored securely via Supabase Authentication.

Location and Organization Data
We store information about your business location(s), including location name, a shared access code used to unlock the mobile app for staff, and your bottle catalog (product names and categories you've added).

Staff Information
Managers may add staff members by name so that inventory counts can be attributed to a specific person. Staff members are assigned a numeric PIN, which is hashed before storage using industry-standard encryption — we do not store PINs in plain text, and PINs cannot be retrieved or viewed by anyone, including us. If a staff member needs a new PIN, a manager can generate a new one; the old one is permanently replaced.

Inventory Session Data
We store the results of inventory counts your staff perform — bottle names, quantities counted, the date and time of the count, and which staff member performed it — so managers can review historical counts and export reports.

Camera and Photos
BottleCall's scan-to-count feature requests access to your device's camera solely to photograph bottle labels for identification. Photos are processed in real time through our servers and Anthropic's Claude API, and are not stored by Proof Apps after identification is complete.

Microphone and Voice Audio
BottleCall's voice-count feature requests access to your device's microphone to transcribe spoken bottle names and quantities in real time using Deepgram's speech-to-text service. Audio is streamed for live transcription and is not stored as recordings by Proof Apps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the voice- and scan-based counting features of the App
  • To identify bottles from photographs using AI
  • To transcribe spoken inventory counts using AI
  • To store and organize your location's bottle catalog, staff, and count history
  • To generate exportable inventory reports for managers
  • To authenticate managers and secure the web dashboard
  • To improve the Service and fix issues

We do not use your data for advertising purposes. BottleCall contains no third-party advertising.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances. We share data only with the following service providers who help us operate the Service:

Anthropic API
Bottle photographs are transmitted through our backend to Anthropic's Claude API to power AI bottle identification. This data is processed in accordance with Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy and is not used to train Anthropic's models without explicit consent.

Deepgram
Voice audio is streamed to Deepgram for real-time transcription during voice counts. Deepgram's privacy policy is available at deepgram.com/privacy-policy.

Supabase
Manager accounts, location data, staff records (with hashed PINs), and inventory session data are stored securely using Supabase, which provides database hosting and authentication infrastructure. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or in response to a valid legal request from a governmental authority.

4. Data Storage, Security, and International Transfers

Account, location, staff, and inventory data are stored securely using Supabase with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Staff PINs are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using bcrypt, a one-way cryptographic function, meaning even Proof Apps cannot view or recover an existing PIN.

Bottle photographs and voice audio are never stored on our servers after processing.

International Data Transfers
Your data is stored on servers located in the United States. If you are accessing BottleCall from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

5. Data Retention

We retain manager account data and location data for as long as the account is active. Inventory session history is retained to support historical reporting unless a manager requests its deletion. If you delete your account, your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days of your request.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

BottleCall is a business tool intended for use by adult staff of bars and restaurants. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at support@proofapps.co and we will delete that information promptly.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Managers have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about their account and location
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account and all associated data
  • Remove staff members and their attributed count history at any time via the dashboard

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@proofapps.co. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

Deleting Your Account
BottleCall does not currently offer in-app self-service account deletion. To request deletion of your manager account and associated location data, email support@proofapps.co with the subject line "Delete My Account" and the email address associated with your account. All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
